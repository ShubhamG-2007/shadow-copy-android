Privacy Policy — SHADOW COPY

Last updated: November 2026

SHADOW COPY is an offline 3D game. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

Data collection

We collect no personal data. The app:

  • Does not create accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or any contact details.
  • Does not track you, profile you, or show advertisements.
  • Does not send any information to us or to any third party.
  • Works fully offline; an internet connection is never required, and the app requests no Android permissions.

Locally stored data

Your game progress (unlocked levels, collected shards, best times) and settings are stored only on your own device (in the app's private local storage). This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

The app requests no special Android permissions. On supported devices it may use the vibration motor for haptic feedback if you enable it in the game's settings; this can be turned off at any time inside the game.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the support email listed on the app's Google Play store page.
