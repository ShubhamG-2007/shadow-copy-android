## Full description

```
SHADOW COPY is a 3D action-puzzle adventure set in a dark, abandoned
facility where light and darkness are connected — and your shadow has
secrets of its own.

SUMMON YOUR SHADOW
With one tap, your shadow rises from the floor as a living clone. It
mirrors your movements, follows your orders, holds pressure plates, baits
robot sentinels, and reaches places you cannot be in two places at once.

SOLVE REAL PUZZLES
Every puzzle is solved through actual gameplay, not cutscenes. Coordinate
with your shadow to open sealed doors, cross rising bridges, beat timed
plates, and outsmart patrolling sentinels.

THREE WORLDS
• The Forgotten Facility — learn to move, jump, crouch, and summon.
• The Sunken Vault — levers, bridges, and your first sentinel hunt.
• The Eclipse Spire — vertical climbs, timed plates, and two sentinels.

FEATURES
• Real 3D third-person gameplay with touch controls
• Shadow clone with Mirror, Follow, Stay, and Go-To commands
• Pressure plates, doors, levers, timed mechanisms, checkpoints
• Collectible light shards hidden in every level
• Procedural animation, particle effects, and synthesized music & sound
• Progress saved on your device — no account, no internet needed

HOW TO PLAY
Move with the left joystick, jump with JUMP, summon your shadow with
SHADOW, interact with ACT, and send your shadow to a spot by tapping the
ground. Keep an eye on your shadow energy — a depleted shadow vanishes.

Every light casts a secret. Find yours.
```
